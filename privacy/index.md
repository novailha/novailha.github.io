This site runs no analytics and loads no third-party fonts. The only third-party scripts are on the newsletter page (Brevo and Google reCAPTCHA).

**Contact form.** If you use it, your email, topic and message go to our form provider (Formspree), which forwards them to us. We only use them to reply.

**Newsletter.** If you subscribe, your email address is stored by Brevo and used only for studio updates. You can unsubscribe from any email.

**Hosting.** The site runs on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

**Links.** Steam and social networks have their own privacy policies.
